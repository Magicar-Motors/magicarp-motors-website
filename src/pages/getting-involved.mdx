## Sponsoring

Magicar Motors is a team of friends making the most of life. We are looking for sponsors who also believe in applying friendship and engineering to do something awesome.

You might be a great sponsor if:
- You’re looking to promote local involvement or youth inspiration
- You’re a brand/engineering company looking to reach a younger audience 
- You’re an individual looking to add a sticker or shout out a cause

We love our sponsors, and our sponsors love us! We love doing:
- brand placement on merch
- brand placement on a vehicle
- shoutouts at our community events
